Fire Mission

Mortar and artillery range calculator for War Dogs

In game: right-click the map, choose Mark coordinates, then copy the chat text. Paste it below, or type the numbers in.

A: your mortar

B: target

Set the range on the mortar's RNG scale, then middle-click the map to drop a marker and turn the mortar to face it.

Fire Mission is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of War Dogs.
